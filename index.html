    The books, letters, and tablets are illustrated teaching objects, not reproductions of specific historical documents. Sources support their word histories.

    Map anchors represent languages and regions, not exact origins. Modern borders are a geographic reference. Trails show selected stages; a comparison line is not an ancestry claim.

    Modern device voices approximate historical forms. The same language keeps the same selected voice. Missing voices are not silently replaced by English; language-specific approximations are labelled.
